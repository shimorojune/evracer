Each dot is a lap: filled with notes, hollow with notes off; a cross marks a crash. With "Keep learning" on, every lap tries one note at one corner (3 m of braking point either way, 0.02 lock of line, 5 m earlier power) and keeps it if that corner got quicker on a clean lap, or, while no lap is clean, if the car got further round. Going off moves that corner's braking point earlier; stopping short of it moves it later. Changing the car makes it re-check every note, as the Python notebook does.

Track notes

    The general driver's weights never change: it steers and drives, and the notes nudge it, the way a driver uses braking markers. Braking later keeps the throttle open until the marker; earlier brakes from the new marker. A line note leans the steering from 80 m before the braking point to the apex, only while the tyres on that side keep margin to the edge. An exit note is a 0.3 floor on the pedal that drops the instant either axle slides.

    Aero (applies instantly, mid-lap)
